# Search — 450 combos on 5m NQ, 2019–2026 — 2026-10-07

**Verdict:** Nothing found. Best real combo is *worse* than the average best on noise.

## Setup
| | |
|---|---|
| Tool | `research/search_5m.py` (grid from `research/search.py`) |
| Data | Dukascopy `IDX_AMERICA_E_NQ_100`, 5-minute, 2019-01 → 2026-10, **531,355 bars** |
| Costs | MNQ: $0.62/contract/side + 1 tick slippage per side (cost drag 0.097R at a 1×ATR stop) |
| Grid | 3 trend filters × 5 triggers × 3 sessions × 2 volatility filters × 5 exit sets = 450 |
| Split | In-sample 2019-01 → 2024-06 (371,948 bars) · out-of-sample 2024-06 → 2026-10 (159,407 bars) |
| Qualifying | ≥150 trades in-sample (442 of 450 qualified) |

## Result

Best in-sample: **+0.074R over 1,772 trades**, PF 1.11 —
`donchian20 | ema50>200 | morning | SL 1.5×ATR / TP 3.0×ATR`.
The whole top-10 is the same family (Donchian breakout + 50/200 trend filter + NY morning),
which usually indicates a real effect rather than a single lucky combo.

**Null test (25 block-bootstrapped paths, 24-bar blocks):**

| | expectancy |
|---|---|
| Best on real data | +0.074R |
| Best on noise, mean | **+0.115R** |
| Best on noise, max | +0.275R |
| **p-value** | **0.720** |

The best real combo is below the *typical* best on noise. Searching 450 combos over 7.8 years
produces +0.1R winners from nothing.

**Out-of-sample** (run once, on the in-sample winner): −0.023R over 811 trades, PF 0.97.

## Edge decay — why in-sample looked positive

Same strategy family, by year:

| 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0.171R | −0.035R | +0.108R | +0.085R | +0.071R | −0.004R | −0.041R | −0.015R |

Positive 2019 and 2021–2023, flat-to-negative from 2024. The in-sample window ends mid-2024, so it
captured the good years; the out-of-sample window is entirely in the decayed regime. Each year's
95% CI is about ±0.15R, so no single year is conclusive, but the direction is consistent with both
the IS/OOS split and with intraday breakout edges being widely traded by now.

## Issues
1. The grid's apparent winner is explained by selection, not edge (p = 0.72).
2. Even in-sample, +0.074R has a 95% CI of [+0.009, +0.140] — it never reached the +0.10R needed
   for the attractive prop-EV row.
3. Out-of-sample sits at −0.023R, statistically indistinguishable from the zero-edge baseline
   (−0.015R measured on random entries).

## Next action
Stop searching this family of indicator combos on NQ — three independent searches (hourly 450,
5m 450, plus CBE and PB individually) have now failed. If the prop-EV route is still wanted, it
does not need an edge: the zero-edge baseline (−0.015R) already clears the −0.075R break-even line.
The open question there is whether firm consistency rules survive the geometry, not whether a
better strategy exists.
