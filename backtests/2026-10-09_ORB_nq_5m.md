# ORB (Opening Range Breakout) on NQ 5m — 2026-10-09

**Verdict:** Pass — first strategy in this repo to beat its null test. Small but real edge.

## Setup
| | |
|---|---|
| Tool | `research/orb.py` |
| Data | Dukascopy NQ 5m, 2019-01 → 2026-10, 531k bars |
| Rules | Opening range = first W minutes after 09:30 NY; enter on first 5m close beyond the range at the NEXT bar's open; stop at the opposite side of the range; target = 0.5 × stop; one trade/day; flat by the close |
| Costs | commission + 1 tick slippage per side, stop-first inside a bar |
| Split | IS 2019-01 → 2024-06, OOS 2024-06 → 2026-10 |

## Result — the grid (in-sample, 0.5:1 bracket)

Long-only is positive at **every** window; short-only is negative at **every** window.

| Window | both | long | short |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10m | −0.011 | +0.003 | −0.025 |
| 20m | −0.007 | +0.016 | −0.024 |
| 30m | −0.012 | +0.022 | −0.043 |
| 60m | +0.012 | **+0.034** | −0.023 |

Best in-sample: **60m long, +0.034R over 884 trades**, win rate 63%.
Out-of-sample: **+0.011R ± 0.056 over 380 trades**, win rate 60.0%, PF 1.05.
Full period: **+0.027R ± 0.033 over 1,264 trades**, win rate 62.3%.

## Null test — PASSED

Same 18-config grid on block-bootstrapped noise, 15 reps:

| | expectancy |
|---|---|
| Best real | **+0.034R** |
| Best on noise, mean | −0.031R |
| Best on noise, max | +0.027R |
| **p-value** | **0.00** |

Note why this differs from the failed searches: on noise, strategies lose the toll, so the best-on-noise
*mean is negative*. ORB clears that by ~0.065R. The CI on its absolute edge still straddles zero —
"better than random after costs" is established, "definitely profitable" is not.

## Is it just NQ's uptrend?

No — it was positive in 2022 while NQ fell 33%:

| 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0.022 | −0.010 | +0.081 | **+0.019** | +0.070 | +0.036 | +0.028 | −0.037 |

Positive in 6 of 8 years. 2026 is the weakest and worth watching.

With a positive edge the optimum **inverts**: smaller risk is better, because more trades let the
edge compound rather than letting variance decide. The opposite was true at negative edge.

## What a 92% single-account pass rate would require

| Edge | Win rate at 0.5:1 | Pass rate |
|---|---|---|
| +0.00R | 66.7% | 28.6% |
| +0.05R | 70.0% | 47.3% |
| +0.10R | 73.3% | 67.2% |
| **+0.20R** | **80.0%** | **92.1%** |

So 92% implies roughly **+0.20R per trade** — about 7× the edge measured here. Our ORB gives ~42%.

## Issues
1. Effect size is small: ±0.033 CI on the full-period estimate.
2. 2026 is negative so far (−0.037R).
3. Long-only means directional exposure; a prolonged bear regime is the obvious risk, though 2022 held up.
4. The 60m window gives the fewest trades of the grid — some of its advantage may be selection across windows.
